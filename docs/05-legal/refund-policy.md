# Refund / Cancellation Policy — Clause Checklist

- Trial terms (see Free/Trial tier in
  [system-architecture.md § Billing](../02-architecture/system-architecture.md#billing))
- Cancellation process and effective date
- Pro-ration (if any) for mid-cycle downgrades/cancellations
- Refund eligibility windows, if any — commercial decision, not
  specified in the PRD
- Data retention/export window after cancellation before deletion (align
  with [../04-privacy/deletion-policy.md](../04-privacy/deletion-policy.md)
  so customers know exactly how long they have to export before real
  deletion triggers)

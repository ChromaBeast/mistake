# Terms of Service — Clause Checklist

See [README.md](./README.md) — this is a checklist for legal counsel,
not usable contract text.

- Service description (link to [PRD.md](../01-product/PRD.md) for what
  Mistake does and its MVP scope/non-goals)
- Account eligibility and responsibilities
- Acceptable use (see [acceptable-use-policy.md](./acceptable-use-policy.md))
- Subscription tiers, billing, and cancellation (see
  [refund-policy.md](./refund-policy.md) and
  [system-architecture.md § Billing](../02-architecture/system-architecture.md#billing)
  for the pricing hypothesis — note these are hypotheses, not committed
  pricing, until commercially finalized)
- Intellectual property (customer data ownership vs. Mistake's IP in the
  product itself)
- AI-generated content disclaimers (see [ai-terms.md](./ai-terms.md))
- Findings are recommendations, not professional financial/legal/audit
  advice — disclaimer needed given the product surfaces financial
  discrepancy figures
- Limitation of liability
- Termination and data deletion on termination (see
  [../04-privacy/deletion-policy.md](../04-privacy/deletion-policy.md))
- Governing law and dispute resolution (India-first market — confirm
  jurisdiction with counsel)
- Changes to terms notice process

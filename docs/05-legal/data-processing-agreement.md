  [../08-compliance/subprocessors.md](../08-compliance/subprocessors.md))

  than restate)
- Data subject request handling process (see
  [../04-privacy/privacy-policy.md](../04-privacy/privacy-policy.md))

  confirmation flagged in
  [dpdp-compliance.md](../04-privacy/dpdp-compliance.md))

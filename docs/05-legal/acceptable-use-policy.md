# Acceptable Use Policy — Clause Checklist

- No uploading of data the uploader isn't authorized to process (e.g.
  another company's records without authorization)
- No attempts to circumvent tenant isolation or access another tenant's
  data
- No attempts to prompt-inject or otherwise manipulate the AI pipeline
  (see [../06-ai/prompt-security.md](../06-ai/prompt-security.md)) —
  worth an explicit clause since this is a documented threat vector
- No reselling or white-labeling access without a separate agreement
- No use of the service for unlawful purposes
- Consequences: suspension, termination, referenced back to
  [terms-of-service.md](./terms-of-service.md)

# Privacy Policy (Draft)

> **Status: internal draft, not for publication.** This describes actual
> product behavior as a starting point for legal counsel to turn into a
> binding public Privacy Policy. Do not publish as-is.

## What we collect
Account information (name, email) for users at customer tenants, and
business records uploaded by tenants (which may incidentally contain
personal data of individuals named in those records — see
[data-processing-policy.md](./data-processing-policy.md)).

## Why we collect it
To provide the Mistake service to the uploading tenant: extraction,
entity resolution, contradiction detection, and financial impact
reporting. See [data-processing-policy.md](./data-processing-policy.md).

## Legal basis / consent
To be finalized with legal counsel per [dpdp-compliance.md](./dpdp-compliance.md)
— depends on the tenant relationship structure (B2B contract vs. direct
consent flows).

## Retention
Per the tenant's configured policy, defaulting to 1 year — see
[data-retention-policy.md](./data-retention-policy.md).

## Deletion
Real deletion, not soft-delete — see [deletion-policy.md](./deletion-policy.md).
Users can request deletion via the in-product Privacy Center.

## Sharing
Only with listed subprocessors necessary to run the service — see
[subprocessor-policy.md](./subprocessor-policy.md) and
[../08-compliance/subprocessors.md](../08-compliance/subprocessors.md).
Customer data is not used to train Mistake's general AI models (see
[data-processing-policy.md](./data-processing-policy.md#customer-data-and-model-training)).

## Your rights
Access, correction, deletion, and export, via the Privacy Center.
Grievance/contact channel to be defined with legal counsel.
